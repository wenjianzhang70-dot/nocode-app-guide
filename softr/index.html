Softr Guide: Portals, Memberships, and Internal Tools Without Code

Softr is a no-code platform for building client portals, membership sites, intranets, directories, and simple CRMs on top of your data. You design pages with blocks, connect a data source, and control who sees what with sign-in and user groups.

What you can build with Softr

  • Client portals: each client signs in and sees only their own projects, files, or invoices.
  • Internal tools and intranets: directories, dashboards, approval workflows.
  • Membership and community sites: gated content for paying or approved members.
  • Simple CRMs and trackers on top of Airtable, Notion, Google Sheets, HubSpot, or Softr's own databases.

Who Softr is for

  • Agencies and service businesses that need a branded portal for clients.
  • Operations and HR teams that repeat the same request workflow.
  • Anyone whose data already lives in Airtable or Notion and who wants a nicer front door for it.

How to get started

  1. Create a free account on the official Softr website.
  2. Pick a template (portal, directory, CRM) or start from a blank app.
  3. Connect your data source or use Softr Databases.
  4. Set up user groups and permissions: who can view, edit, or see nothing.
  5. Publish on a Softr subdomain, then add a custom domain on a paid plan.

Strengths and limits

Strengths: predictable flat pricing; a genuinely usable free tier; permissions built for external users, not just teammates; templates get you to a working portal fast.

Limits: pricing scales with user counts, so very large public audiences get expensive; it is not a full project-management tool out of the box; highly specialized needs (for example trust accounting) may need integrations or workarounds.
