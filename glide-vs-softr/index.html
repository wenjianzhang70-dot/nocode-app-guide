Glide vs Softr: Which One Should You Pick?

There is no single winner. Glide and Softr overlap — both build apps without code — but they were designed for different jobs. Pick by your scenario, not by a feature checklist.

At a glance

GlideSoftr
Best atData-driven mobile apps and internal tools from spreadsheetsGated portals, membership sites, and intranets
Natural data homeGoogle Sheets, Excel, Glide TablesAirtable, Notion, Google Sheets, Softr Databases
PermissionsSign-in and row-level accessUser groups built for clients and external users
Free planGood for learning; publishing is limitedUsable: unlimited apps, 5 app users
How cost scalesUsers, rows, and updatesMostly user counts
Watch out forTwo product lines (GlideOS vs Classic) with separate pricingLarge public audiences get expensive

Pick by scenario

  • Your data is in Google Sheets and you want a mobile app for your team — pick Glide.
  • You need a client portal where each client sees only their own stuff — pick Softr.
  • You are building a membership or community site with gated content — pick Softr.
  • You want an AI-assisted build from a spreadsheet or a prompt — look at GlideOS (Glide's newer product).
  • You have a defined list of external users and want predictable cost — pick Softr.
  • You need a public directory anyone can open — either works; compare the free tiers first.

Our take

If you forced us to choose one starting point: start with Softr when the app has an audience that signs in (clients, members, staff), and start with Glide when the app is a tool your team uses against a spreadsheet. In both cases, build one real app on the free plan before paying — the limits that matter only show up with your own data.
