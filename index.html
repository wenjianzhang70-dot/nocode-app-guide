No-Code App Builders: A Practical Guide

Does this sound familiar? Your business runs on spreadsheets, but every time someone needs a number, they dig through tabs for half an hour. Or clients keep asking for updates, and the answers live in five different places. No-code app builders exist for exactly that frustration: they turn a spreadsheet or a database into a working app — a client portal, an internal tool, a directory, a simple CRM — without writing code. This guide covers the two tools we recommend looking at first, Glide and Softr, compares them head to head, and explains how to pick.

Start here: which one fits you?

Your data lives in Google Sheets and you want a mobile-friendly app fast?
Start with Glide. It was built around spreadsheets and is strongest for data-driven mobile apps and internal tools.

You need a client portal, membership site, or intranet with sign-in and permissions?
Start with Softr. It is strongest for gated portals on top of Airtable, Notion, or its own databases.
